How grading works at Ghazi University

Ghazi University uses absolute grading with four passing grades: A, B, C and D. F is the failing grade. The rules come from the Ghazi University, Dera Ghazi Khan, Statutes and Regulations, 2021 for first-degree courses such as BS, B.Sc. (Hons) and BBA.

Unlike universities where every B is worth the same, Ghazi University gives quality points mark by mark. Each credit hour of a course is worth 20 marks, so a 3-credit course is marked out of 60. The official quality point table then gives a slightly higher number of points for every extra mark you get, up to 80%. That is why this calculator asks for your marks instead of just your letter grade.

Ghazi University grade and quality point table

GradeMarksGrade points per credit hour
A80% – 100%4.00
B65% – 79%3.00 – 3.93
C50% – 64%2.00 – 2.93
D40% – 49%1.00 – 1.90
FBelow 40%0.00

Inside each grade the points follow the official table step by step:

  • 40% to 50%: 1.00 point at 40%, plus 0.10 for every extra 1%. So 45% is 1.50.
  • 50% to 80%: 2.00 points at 50%, plus 1/15 of a point for every extra 1%. So 55% is 2.33, 65% is 3.00 and 70% is 3.33.
  • 80% and above: always 4.00.

Your quality points for a course are its credit hours × these grade points. For example, 45 out of 60 in a 3-credit course is 75%, which gives 3.67 × 3 = 11.00 quality points, exactly as in the official table.

How to calculate your Ghazi University GPA: worked example

This is the example given in the Statutes and Regulations 2021:

Course II345 / 60 (75%)B11.00
Course III348 / 60 (80%)A12.00
Course IV338 / 60 (63.3%)C8.67
Course V228 / 40 (70%)B6.67
Total1346.34

GPA = 46.34 ÷ 13 = 3.56

Your CGPA works the same way across all semesters: add up the quality points of every semester and divide by the total credit hours. The CGPA tab does this from your semester GPAs.

Calculator tip: if your result card shows quality points instead of marks, switch to Grade points and type the quality points ÷ credit hours. For Course II above, that is 11.00 ÷ 3 = 3.67.

Good standing, probation and minimum CGPA

  • First semester: you need a GPA of at least 1.00 to be promoted to the second semester.
  • Second semester onwards: if your CGPA falls below 1.50, you are dropped from the rolls of the university. You can be re-admitted once, in the first semester of the next session.
  • To get your degree you need a CGPA of at least 2.20 with no F grade.
  • CGPA between 1.90 and 2.20 after the 8th semester: if you have not repeated any course, you may repeat some of your lowest-grade courses once to reach 2.20.

The Target tab shows the GPA you need in your remaining semesters to reach 2.20 or any other CGPA.

Repeating a course and improving a D grade

  • If you get an F, you must repeat the course when it is offered again.
  • If you get a D, you may repeat the course once to improve it. Courses passed with A, B or C cannot be repeated.
  • You can repeat or improve at most six courses (18 credit hours) in your whole degree, and not more than one course per Fall or Spring semester.
  • The improved grade replaces the F or D in your CGPA, but the old grade still appears on your transcript.

Calculator tip: for a repeated course, enter only your new, better marks.

Ghazi University CGPA to percentage

The Statutes and Regulations 2021 do not include a formula for turning CGPA into a percentage. The Percentage tab reads the official quality point table backwards to show the average marks your CGPA stands for:

  • CGPA 2.00 to 3.99: percentage = 50 + (CGPA − 2) × 15. So 3.56 is about 73.4%.
  • CGPA 1.00 to 1.99: percentage = 40 + (CGPA − 1) × 10.
  • CGPA 4.00: 80% or more.

Because this is not an official university formula, check which method an employer or university accepts before you use it on a form.

GU CGPA calculator FAQs

What are the grades and marks at Ghazi University?

A is 80% to 100%, B is 65% to 79%, C is 50% to 64% and D is 40% to 49%. Below 40% is an F, the failing grade.

Why do two B grades give different grade points at Ghazi University?

Ghazi University gives quality points mark by mark. Inside the B range, 65% is 3.00 points and 79% is 3.93 points, so your exact marks matter, not just the letter.

What CGPA do I need to get my degree from Ghazi University?

You need a CGPA of at least 2.20 with no F grade. If you finish the 8th semester with a CGPA from 1.90 to below 2.20 and have not repeated any course, you may repeat some low-grade courses once to reach 2.20.

When are you dropped from Ghazi University for low CGPA?

From the second semester onwards, a CGPA below 1.50 means you are dropped from the rolls. In the first semester you need a GPA of at least 1.00 to be promoted.

Can I improve a D grade at Ghazi University?

Yes. You can repeat a D-grade course once. The better grade is used in your CGPA, but the D still appears on your transcript. You can repeat or improve at most six courses (18 credit hours) in the whole degree.

How do I convert my Ghazi University CGPA to a percentage?

There is no official formula in the Statutes and Regulations 2021. Reading the quality point table backwards, a CGPA from 2.00 to 3.99 equals 50 + (CGPA − 2) × 15, so 3.56 is about 73.4%. Check which method the employer or university accepts.

My result shows marks out of 60, not a percentage. What do I enter?

Divide your marks by the course total and multiply by 100. Each credit hour is worth 20 marks, so 45 out of 60 in a 3-credit course is 75%.
