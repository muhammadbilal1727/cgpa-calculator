Stanford quick facts

  • Stanford uses a 4.3 scale: an A+ is worth 4.3, an A is 4.0 and an A- is 3.7.
  • An NP counts as 0.0 and its units stay in the total, so it pulls your GPA down.
  • A temporary L grade counts as 2.0 until the final grade is in.

How Stanford calculates your GPA

Stanford gives grade points only to letter grades, and it uses a 4.3 scale. Each course's grade points are multiplied by its units, so a 5-unit A is worth 20 grade points. Your GPA is the total of those grade points divided by the total units of all courses graded A+ through NP.

No other grades or symbols are used in the GPA: +, CR, *, GNR, RP, N, N-, W, NC, S and I are all left out. An NP, on the other hand, is worth 0.0 and its units still count, so an A (4 units) and an NP (4 units) give a GPA of 2.0, not 4.0.

Can a Stanford GPA be above 4.0?

Yes. Because an A+ is worth 4.3, a student with many A+ grades can have a GPA above 4.0. If a form asks for a GPA "out of 4.0", give your GPA as it appears on your transcript and mention that Stanford uses a 4.3 scale. Stanford does not publish a percentage formula; the converter tab gives a simple estimate, (GPA ÷ 4.3) × 100.

Stanford CGPA calculator FAQs

Is an A+ worth 4.3 at Stanford?

Yes. Stanford uses a 4.3 scale: A+ = 4.3, A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3 and so on down to D- = 0.7 and NP = 0.0.

Does an NP count in my Stanford GPA?

Yes. NP is worth 0.0 grade points and its units stay in the total, so it lowers your GPA. An A in 4 units plus an NP in 4 units gives 2.0.

Do CR/NC courses count in the Stanford GPA?

No. CR, NC, S, W, I and the other symbols are not used in calculating the GPA.

What is an L grade at Stanford?

An L is a temporary grade. It counts as 2.0 grade points until the final grade is submitted.

Can my Stanford GPA be higher than 4.0?

Yes, because an A+ is worth 4.3. When a form asks for a GPA out of 4.0, give your transcript GPA and note that Stanford uses a 4.3 scale.

How do I convert my Stanford GPA to a percentage?

Stanford has no official formula. A simple estimate is (GPA ÷ 4.3) × 100, so 3.80 is about 88.37%. Mark it as an estimate.
