GPA কীভাবে হিসাব করা হয়?

GPA = (Σ ক্রেডিট × গ্রেড পয়েন্ট) ÷ (Σ ক্রেডিট)

CGPA কীভাবে হিসাব করা হয়?

CGPA = (Σ সেমিস্টার GPA × সেমিস্টার ক্রেডিট) ÷ (মোট ক্রেডিট)

বেশি ক্রেডিটের সেমিস্টার বেশি গুরুত্ব পায়। উদাহরণ: 18 ক্রেডিটে 3.50 এবং 15 ক্রেডিটে 3.00 → (3.5×18 + 3×15) ÷ 33 = 108 ÷ 33 = 3.27

UTM quick facts

  • Marks 80 and above (A and A+) both give 4.00.
  • Generally D+ (40–44) is the minimum pass, but your faculty can set a higher one.
  • Improving a grade (B- or lower, once, up to 15 credits): the better grade counts in your PNGK.

How grading works at UTM

Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) measures your results with two numbers: PNG, the grade point average for one semester, and PNGK, the cumulative average over all semesters. Each is the total grade value (grade value × credits) divided by the total credits counted.

PNGK = total grade value of all semesters ÷ total credits counted in all semesters

Academic standing at UTM

StatusPNGK
Good Standing (KB)2.00 or more
Conditional Standing (KS)1.70 to below 2.00
Failed Standing (KG), dismissedbelow 1.70

You need Good Standing (KB) to be awarded your degree. A semester PNG of 3.67 or more (with at least 12 credits) earns a Dean’s List certificate, and graduating with a PNGK of 3.67 or more earns the Dean’s Award medal.

Failed courses and grade improvement

If you fail a core or university course, you must take it again until you pass, and the PNGK then uses the credits and grade value of your latest attempt; the original attempt is removed. With your faculty's approval you can also improve a course you passed with B- or lower. You may do this once per course and for up to 15 credits during your studies, and the better of the two grades counts in your PNGK.

UTM CGPA calculator FAQs

Are A+ and A both 4.00 at UTM?

Yes. A+ (90–100 marks) and A (80–89) are both worth 4.00. A- (75–79) is 3.67.

What is the passing grade at UTM?

In general D+ (40–44 marks) is the minimum pass, but your faculty can set a different passing grade for a course.

What PNGK do I need to graduate from UTM?

You need Good Standing (KB), which means a PNGK of 2.00 or more.

What is the Dean’s Award at UTM?

A semester PNG of 3.67 or more with at least 12 credits earns a Dean’s List certificate. Graduating with a PNGK of 3.67 or more earns the Dean’s Award medal.

If I improve a grade at UTM, which one counts?

For courses passed with B- or lower, you may improve the grade once per course, up to 15 credits in total. The better of the original and new grade counts in your PNGK.

What happens if my PNGK falls below 2.00?

From 1.70 to below 2.00 you are in Conditional Standing (KS). Below 1.70 you are in Failed Standing (KG) and dismissed.
