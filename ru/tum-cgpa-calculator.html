How is the grade average calculated?

Average = (Σ ECTS × grade) ÷ (Σ ECTS)

Example: a 6-ECTS module with 1.3 and a 9-ECTS module with 2.7 → (6×1.3 + 9×2.7) ÷ 15 = 32.1 ÷ 15 = 2.14. TUM keeps one decimal without rounding: 2.1.

Как рассчитывается CGPA?

CGPA = (Σ GPA семестра × кредиты семестра) ÷ (всего кредитов)

Семестры с большим числом кредитов весят больше. Пример: 3.50 при 18 кредитах и 3.00 при 15 кредитах → (3.5×18 + 3×15) ÷ 33 = 108 ÷ 33 = 3.27

Как это рассчитывается?

Average needed = (Target × total ECTS − Current × completed ECTS) ÷ remaining ECTS

Example: an average of 2.4 after 90 ECTS, a target of 2.0 and 90 ECTS left → (2.0 × 180 − 2.4 × 90) ÷ 90 = 1.60 or better

TUM quick facts

  • German grades: 1.0 is the best, 4.0 the lowest pass; 4.3, 4.7 and 5.0 are fails.
  • Your overall grade (Gesamtnote) is weighted by ECTS credits, including the thesis module.
  • An overall grade up to 1.2 means “mit Auszeichnung bestanden” (passed with distinction).

How grading works at TUM

The General Academic and Examination Regulations of the Technical University of Munich (APSO) use the German scale from 1 to 5. Examiners can raise or lower a grade by 0.3, but 0.7 and 5.3 are not allowed, so the possible grades are 1.0, 1.3, 1.7, 2.0, 2.3, 2.7, 3.0, 3.3, 3.7, 4.0, 4.3, 4.7 and 5.0. Anything worse than 4.0 is a fail.

When several grades are averaged, for example module part exams, TUM keeps one decimal place and drops the rest without rounding: 2.14 and 2.19 both become 2.1. This calculator shows TUM averages the same way.

TUM overall grade (Gesamtnote)

Your overall grade is the weighted average of all your modules, including the thesis (Abschlussarbeit). Each module counts according to its credits. Your programme's own regulations (FPSO) can set different weights or leave out the first study phase, so check your FPSO too. Modules where you only completed coursework without a grade don't count.

Converting your CGPA from abroad: the Bavarian formula

If you studied outside Germany, for example in Pakistan or India, TUM and most German universities convert your grade with the modified Bavarian formula recommended by the Kultusministerkonferenz (KMK):

German grade = 1 + 3 × (Nmax − Nd) ÷ (Nmax − Nmin)

Nmax is the best grade in your system, Nmin the lowest passing grade and Nd your own grade. For a CGPA of 3.20 on a 4.0 scale where the lowest pass is 2.0: 1 + 3 × (4 − 3.2) ÷ (4 − 2) = 2.2. If the lowest pass in your system is 1.0, the same CGPA gives 1 + 3 × 0.8 ÷ 3 = 1.8. That is why it matters which lowest passing grade you use: check your transcript or degree regulations. The Bavarian formula tab does this for four common scales.

TUM CGPA calculator FAQs

Is 1.0 the best grade at TUM?

Yes. German grades run from 1.0 (best) to 4.0 (lowest pass). 4.3, 4.7 and 5.0 are fail grades.

How is the TUM overall grade (Gesamtnote) calculated?

As the weighted average of all modules, including the thesis, with each module weighted by its credits. Your FPSO can set different weights.

Does TUM round the grade average?

When grades are averaged, the APSO keeps one decimal place and drops the rest without rounding, so 2.19 becomes 2.1.

What grade do I need for “mit Auszeichnung” at TUM?

An overall grade of 1.2 or better. 1.3 to 1.5 is “sehr gut”, 1.6 to 2.5 “gut”, 2.6 to 3.5 “befriedigend” and 3.6 to 4.0 “bestanden”.

How does TUM convert my CGPA from Pakistan or India?

With the modified Bavarian formula: 1 + 3 × (best grade − your grade) ÷ (best grade − lowest passing grade). TUM keeps one decimal and does not round to the usual grade steps.

Can I improve a passed module grade at TUM?

Only if your programme’s FPSO allows it. Where it does, you can repeat a passed module exam once and the better result counts.
