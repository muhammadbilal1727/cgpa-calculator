CGPA কীভাবে হিসাব করা হয়?

CGPA = (Σ সেমিস্টার GPA × সেমিস্টার ক্রেডিট) ÷ (মোট ক্রেডিট)

UM quick facts

  • Both A+ (90–100) and A (80–89.99) are worth 4.0.
  • C (50%) is the lowest pass; C-, D+, D and F are fail grades.
  • Honours with distinction needs a final CGPA of 3.70 and a clean record (no F, no repeats).

How grading works at Universiti Malaya

Universiti Malaya (UM) grades every course with a letter grade linked to a mark range and a grade point. Your semester GPA and your CGPA are the usual credit-weighted averages: multiply each course's grade point by its credit hours, add them up and divide by the total credit hours.

Two things make UM's scale different from many others. First, both A+ and A are worth 4.0, so marks above 80 all give the full 4.0. Second, the lowest passing grade is C (50%). C-, D+ and D still carry grade points, but they are fail grades.

UM degree award: honours and distinction

UM confers an honours degree based on your final CGPA, and you need a final CGPA of at least 2.00 to qualify. You receive a Pass with Honours (With Distinction) if you:

  • finish with a CGPA of 3.70 or above;
  • have never received an F for any course during your programme;
  • have never repeated a failed course or taken a course again to improve its grade; and
  • complete your programme within the minimum or prescribed period.

So a 3.75 CGPA with a repeated course does not earn the distinction. UM does not publish a CGPA-to-percentage formula; if a form asks for one, attach your transcript.

UM CGPA calculator FAQs

Is an A+ worth more than an A at UM?

No. Both A+ (90–100 marks) and A (80–89.99 marks) are worth 4.0 grade points at Universiti Malaya.

What is the passing grade at Universiti Malaya?

C, which is 50.00–54.99 marks and 2.0 grade points. C-, D+, D and F are fail grades, even though C-, D+ and D carry some grade points.

What CGPA do I need for honours with distinction at UM?

A final CGPA of 3.70 or above. You must also have no F grades, no repeated failed or improvement courses, and finish within the prescribed period.

What is the minimum CGPA to graduate from UM?

You need a final CGPA of at least 2.00 to be awarded an honours degree.

What grade is 75 marks at UM?

A- (75.00–79.99), worth 3.7 grade points and described as a distinction.

How do I convert my UM CGPA to a percentage?

UM does not publish an official CGPA-to-percentage formula. If a form asks for a percentage, attach your official transcript, which shows your grades and the grading scheme.
