What you can write to us about

  • A result that looks wrong. Tell us which grading scale you chose, the numbers you entered and the result you expected.
  • Adding your university's grading scale. Send us a link to your university's official grading policy and we'll take a look.
  • Ideas and feedback. A feature you'd like, or something that was confusing.
  • Privacy questions. Anything about how the website handles data.
  • Partnerships and advertising. If you'd like to work with us.

Before you write

Your question may already be answered in our frequently asked questions. Please don't send transcripts, ID documents, passwords or other sensitive information by email. We never need them to help you.

We can't confirm official results

CGPA Calculator is an independent website. For your official CGPA, percentage or transcript, please contact your university's examination or registrar office.
