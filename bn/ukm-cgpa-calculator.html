GPA কীভাবে হিসাব করা হয়?

GPA = (Σ ক্রেডিট × গ্রেড পয়েন্ট) ÷ (Σ ক্রেডিট)

উদাহরণ: গণিত 3 ক্রেডিট ও 4.00 গ্রেড পয়েন্ট, ইংরেজি 2 ক্রেডিট ও 3.00 → (3×4 + 2×3) ÷ (3+2) = 18 ÷ 5 = 3.60

CGPA কীভাবে হিসাব করা হয়?

CGPA = (Σ সেমিস্টার GPA × সেমিস্টার ক্রেডিট) ÷ (মোট ক্রেডিট)

বেশি ক্রেডিটের সেমিস্টার বেশি গুরুত্ব পায়। উদাহরণ: 18 ক্রেডিটে 3.50 এবং 15 ক্রেডিটে 3.00 → (3.5×18 + 3×15) ÷ 33 = 108 ÷ 33 = 3.27

UKM quick facts

  • A (80–100) is the top grade at 4.00; there is no A+ at UKM.
  • A PNGK of 3.67–4.00 gives a degree with Honours (Cemerlang).
  • If you repeat a course, only the latest grade counts in your PNGK.

PNG and PNGK at UKM

Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM) calls the semester GPA PNG (Purata Nilai Gred) and the CGPA PNGK (Purata Nilai Gred Kumulatif). Both are credit-weighted averages: multiply each course's grade value by its credit hours, add up the results and divide by the total credit hours you took.

The worked example in the UKM regulations divides 77.98 by 27 and gives a PNGK of 2.88. Since 77.98 ÷ 27 is 2.888…, the result is cut after two decimals rather than rounded, and this calculator shows UKM results the same way.

UKM CGPA calculator FAQs

What are PNG and PNGK at UKM?

PNG (Purata Nilai Gred) is your semester GPA and PNGK (Purata Nilai Gred Kumulatif) is your CGPA across all semesters. Both are credit-weighted averages.

What PNGK do I need for a Cemerlang degree at UKM?

A PNGK of 3.67 to 4.00 gives a Bachelor’s degree with Honours (Cemerlang). From 2.00 to 3.66 it is a Bachelor’s degree with Honours.

Is there an A+ at UKM?

No. A (80–100 marks) is the top grade, worth 4.00. A- (75–79) is 3.67.

What is the passing grade at UKM?

C (50–54 marks) is the lowest pass. C-, D+, D and E are fail grades.

If I repeat a course at UKM, which grade counts?

Only the latest grade is used to calculate your PNGK.

What happens if my PNGK is below 2.00?

From 1.00 to below 2.00 your status is Lulus Bersyarat (conditional pass) and you are not eligible to graduate. Below 1.00 the status is fail and dismissed.
